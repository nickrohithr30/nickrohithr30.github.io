How Concentrations of Certain Pesticides in Wells and Surface Water Correlate with Total Pesticides Used by each California County

Hello everyone, my name is Nikhil Ravishankar. I've done a project on the usage of pesticides in California. The project focuses on an herbicide called
"Atrazine", and an insecticide called "Imidacloprid".

Atrazine

Atrazine is an herbicide that is mainly used for preventing weeds and grasses from growing. Atrazine dissolves in water. Some of the crops that
atrazine are used on are sorghum, sugarcane, corn, macadamia and pineapple. It is also used for regrowing evergreen forests. It can get into
waterways like lakes and streams by being washed away from soil by rain. It can get travel deep into the soil and get into the groundwater.
For this herbicide to work, it first needs to dissolve in water and then be absorbed by the roots of the weeds and grasses. Once it is
by the weeds and grasses, it gets into into the shoots and leaves of the weeds and grasses and prevents photosynthesis from happening. It can
be applied to crops as a powder, as a liquid or in a granular form.


Imidacloprid

Imidacloprid is a neonicotinoid insecticide. It weakens the central nervous systems of insects. Turf, sugar beets, corn, cereal, potatoes, rice
and cotton are some crops that this pesticide is used on. This insecticide can go from soil into the groundwater. It is allowed to be used as a
termiticide and a preservative on wood. It is allowed to be used on residential gardens and lawns. There are farm workers who have claimed that
they experienced vomitting, eye irritation, breathlessness, skin irritation, eye irritation, dizziness and confusion after they were exposed to
pesticides that have imidacloprid in them. This insecticide is less toxic to birds and mammals than it is to insects.

The project consists of 2 maps. The maps are located below. The first map shows 4 things. The first is how much pesticides each county in California used
each year from 2017 to 2023. The second thing it shows is which counties use the most pesticides and which counties use the least amount of pesticides. The third
thing it shows is the variation in average well concentration of atrazine in each county from 2017 to 2023. Lastly, the map shows the variation in average surface
water concentration of imidacloprid in each county from 2017 to 2023.

The second map also shows the variaton inaverage surface water concentration of imidaclprid in each county in California from 2017 to 2023. However, the average is
displayed differently than how it is shown on the first map. The first map displayed the variation in average imidacloprid concentration by circle size. On the
second map, the variation in average concentration is shown by the change in the shade of green. The lighter the green the lower the average concentration, and
the darker the green the higher the average concentration.

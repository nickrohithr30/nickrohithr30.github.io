Hello everyone, my name is Nikhil Ravishankar. I've done a project on the usage of pesticides in California. The project focuses on an herbicide called
"Atrazine", and an insecticide called "Imidacloprid".

The project consists of 2 maps. The maps are located below. The first map shows 4 things. The first is how much pesticides each county in California used
each year from 2017 to 2023. The second thing it shows is which counties use the most pesticides and which counties use the least amount of pesticides. The third
thing it shows is the average well concentration of anAtrazine in each county from 2017 to 2023. Lastly, the map shows the average surface water concentration of
an insecticide Imidacloprid in each county from 2017 to 2023.
